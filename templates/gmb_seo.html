Google My Business (GMB) SEO

Google My Business SEO is essential for local businesses aiming to improve their visibility in local search results and Google Maps.

Your customers are searching for businesses like yours on Google every day—make sure they find you first. At Clickcatcher, we help businesses stand out on local search with expert Google My Business (GMB) optimization.

GMB is one of the most powerful (and free) tools to improve your visibility on Google Search and Maps. But simply creating a listing isn’t enough. To truly rank and attract local customers, your profile needs to be optimized, active, and strategically managed.

Claim and Verify Your Listing

Start by claiming and verifying your Google My Business profile. This ensures you have full control over your business information, reviews, and updates.

Complete Your Profile

Fill out all the details including business name, address, phone number, website, business hours, and category. The more complete your profile, the better it ranks in local searches.

Use Consistent NAP

Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP) are consistent across your website, GMB, and other online directories. Inconsistencies can harm your SEO.

Choose the Right Categories

Select the most relevant primary and secondary business categories. Categories help Google understand what your business offers and when to show your listing.

Write a Compelling Business Description

Add a keyword-rich and informative business description. Mention what makes your business unique and the services or products you offer.

Add Photos and Videos

Upload high-quality images of your storefront, interior, team, and products. Businesses with photos get more clicks and engagement. Keep them updated regularly.

Collect and Respond to Reviews

Encourage satisfied customers to leave positive reviews. Always respond to reviews — both positive and negative — to show engagement and build trust.

Post Regular Updates

Use GMB’s “Posts” feature to share updates, offers, events, or blog content. Fresh activity signals Google that your business is active, helping with local ranking.

Add Services or Products

Use the Services or Products section to list what you offer, along with prices and descriptions. This improves visibility when users search for specific items.

Use Keywords Strategically

Include relevant local keywords in your business description, services, and posts. For example: “best bakery in Delhi” or “affordable laptop repair in Mumbai”.

Monitor Insights and Performance

Regularly check GMB Insights to track how customers find your business, where they come from, and what actions they take. Use this data to improve your local SEO strategy.
