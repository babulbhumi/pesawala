Privacy Policy

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or use our services.

At Pesa Wala (https://pesawala.com/), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or engage with our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect both personal and non-personal information from users when they visit our website or interact with us. The types of information we may collect include:

Personal Information
  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Account Information: If you create an account or sign up for our services, we may collect additional information like job title, business details, etc.
Non-Personal Information
  • Website Activity: Browsing behavior, pages visited, time spent on our site, clicks, and interactions
  • Technical Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, and cookies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to provide, improve, and personalize your experience on our website. Specifically, we may use your information for:

  • Providing Services: To manage your account, deliver digital marketing services, and respond to inquiries.
  • Personalization: To tailor content and recommendations based on your interests and preferences.
  • Communication: To send you marketing materials, updates, newsletters, and relevant offers.
  • Website Improvement: To monitor and improve our website performance, user experience, and security.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and contractual obligations.

3. How We Protect Your Data

We take the security of your personal data seriously. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience on our website. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us track your preferences and provide a personalized experience.

You can control cookie settings through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

For more information, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share your data with trusted third-party service providers who help us deliver services, such as:

  • Payment processors
  • Email marketing platforms
  • Analytics providers

These third parties are required to handle your data securely and only for the purposes for which we’ve engaged them.

6. Your Data Protection Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: You can request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: You can request the deletion of your personal data (subject to legal restrictions).
  • Objection: You can object to the processing of your data for marketing or other legitimate interests.
  • Data Portability: You can request your data in a structured, commonly used format to transfer it to another service provider.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@pesawala.com.

7. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. These external sites are not governed by this Privacy Policy. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit to understand how they handle your data.

8. Children’s Privacy

Pesa Wala does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under the age of 18, we will take steps to delete that information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. When we make changes, we will update the “Effective Date” at the top of this page.
