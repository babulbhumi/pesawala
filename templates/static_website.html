What is a Static Website?

A static website displays fixed content that does not change unless manually updated. It is ideal for businesses that need a clean, professional online presence without complex backend functionality.

Why Choose One?

Static websites offer many advantages including lightning-fast loading speed, top-level security, low hosting costs, and easy deployment. They are perfect for product showcases, portfolios, and business information sites.
